Stage 1: The Founders

It all starts with a core group. These four individuals share a vision and are tightly connected, forming the nucleus of the network.

In this clique, everyone knows everyone else, creating a high-trust, high-density core.

Stage 2: Early Adopters

Word spreads. Early adopters join the founders. They are mostly connected to the core, expanding the network's reach but still relying on the founders for information flow.

The network grows from 4 to 16 members, with the founders becoming central hubs.

Stage 3: Emergent Communities

As the network scales, specialized communities emerge. Engineering, Marketing, and Sales clusters form, each with internal high density but limited external connections.

Homophily takes over: people connect with those similar to themselves, leading to a fragmented social landscape.

Stage 4: Strategic Bridges

The "Strength of Weak Ties" becomes apparent. Strategic individuals act as bridges between the isolated communities.

Alice connects with Engineering; Bob syncs with Marketing. These sparse connections are vital for the flow of novel information across the whole organization.

Stage 5: The Full Ecosystem

Finally, the network reaches critical mass. Hundreds of connections now span the entire space, creating a "Small World" effect where anyone is only a few hops away from anyone else.

A robust, resilient, and highly connected social ecosystem is born.
