Parallel Coordinates Strum

Selected: 2,000 / 2,000 (100%)
Alt+Drag between axes to see relationship in scatterplot
Exploration Guide:
  • Angular Selection: Hold Alt and drag between two axes to create a Strum. Only lines intersecting the strum are selected.
  • Range Selection: Click and drag on an axis to filter by value range.
  • Intersection: Multiple brushes (strums and range) are combined using AND logic.
  • Linking: The scatterplot automatically focuses on the dimensions being strummed.
  • Reset: Click a strum line to remove it; click background to clear axis brushes.
