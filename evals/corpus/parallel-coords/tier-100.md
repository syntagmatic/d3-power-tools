Parallel coordinates with 6 axes plus a sparkline summary table. Dataset: 150 synthetic cities, columns population, temp, elevation, rainfall, gdp, density, plus a region label across 6 regions. Brushing on either view filters both. Table shows per-axis min, median, max plus a distribution sparkline for the brushed subset, with a live count badge. Stay readable when only three or four cities pass the brush.
